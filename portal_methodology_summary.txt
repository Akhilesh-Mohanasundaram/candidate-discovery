Our system implements a hybrid, rule-aware ranking architecture specifically designed to capture nuance beyond keyword matching. 

We process candidates through a rigorous multi-stage pipeline:
1. Hard/Soft Veto Filtering: We parse the JD to extract firm boundaries (e.g., pure academic researchers, LangChain-only experience) and immediately eliminate non-viable candidates.
2. Honeypot Detection: We utilize 7 explicit trap detection logic rules (e.g., Timeline Overlap, Fake Experts) to zero-score hallucinated or impossible profiles.
3. Semantic Skill Matching (35%): We employ a pre-computed MiniLM-L6-v2 embedding model to measure semantic similarity between JD requirements and candidate skills, weighted heavily by their proficiency level and actual duration of usage.
4. Career Quality & Logistics (50%): We balance raw skill scores with real-world career trajectory mapping (titles, companies) and geographical feasibility.
5. Behavioral Multiplier (15%): The raw score is finally scaled [0.3-1.0] by a multi-signal availability index derived from the candidate's last active date, recruiter response rate, and profile completeness.

This approach guarantees that our top 100 shortlist is not just technically aligned but actually hireable, engaged, and structurally sound.
